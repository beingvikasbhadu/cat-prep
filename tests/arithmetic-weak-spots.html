Your 15 arithmetic weak spots

Every card here comes from a mistake in your own tests. Each one starts with a short story question. Try it first, then check: the card opens with the fix, the rule and an example. About 45 minutes. Then take the 15-question test at the end.

How to use each card
  1. Read the story and solve it on paper. Type your answer and press Check.
  2. Right or wrong, read the card that opens: the rule, what it means, an example and one line to remember.
  3. Wrong? Try again after reading. Stuck for 3 minutes? Press Show me.

3 habits before you type any answer

  1. Reread the last line. Term or sum? Boat or stream? Water added or water after? Sides or angle? You lost marks to this 6 times.
  2. TITA: put the answer back in. There are no options to catch a slip. A count of coins or people must be a whole number.
  3. Rough-check the size. A share can't be bigger than the profit; a 3/4-speed trip can't make you early.
